Customer query:
How do I reset my password?

ASSISTANT ANSWER:
To reset your password, follow these steps:
1. Go to the login page of the website or application.
2. Click on the "Forgot Password" link, usually located below the username field.
3. Enter your registered email address or phone number.
4. Follow the instructions sent to your email.
5. Check your email or phone for a password reset link and click on it.
6. Enter your new password and confirm it.
7. Complete the reset process by clicking "Submit" or "Reset Password.
Your password will be updated.
